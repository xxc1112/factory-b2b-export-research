# Factory B2B Export Research

Produce an executive-ready, source-verified B2B export market research and market-entry strategy report for Chinese factory products, anchored on real product specs, verifiable market sizing, current HS tariffs, real overseas buyers, and auditable unit economics.

## When to Use

- The user wants a B2B export market research and entry strategy report for a Chinese factory product.
- The user provides a 1688, Alibaba, or factory product URL as a reference to define the target hardware category.
- The user needs a decision-grade export plan covering market sizing, target country ranking, HS tariffs, competitors, real overseas B2B buyers, compliance, website funnel, unit economics, and a 90-day launch roadmap.

## Steps

### 1. Anchor Product Scope and Category Boundaries

Inspect any user-provided product reference URL first to confirm the exact product class, core specs (capacity/power/payload/frequency), reference ex-factory price, and gross weight/dimensions.

- Separate the factory's actual exportable product tier from broader industry categories so market sizing, standards, and compliance never mix incompatible tiers (for example, benchtop QC machines vs. heavy aerospace systems, or standalone components vs. complete equipment).
- Flag obvious listing anomalies (such as unit typos in packing dimensions or unverified claims) for factory confirmation instead of copying them blindly.
- Treat any unverified factory capability (certifications, cell/component brands, warranty, defect rate, insurance) as "Pending Factory Confirmation" rather than fact.

### 2. Run Four Parallel Research Tracks

Split external research into four independent workstreams (using parallel subagents when available) and require direct clickable URLs, publication years, currencies, and scope definitions for every external figure:

1. **Market Sizing and Customs Tariffs**: Gather 3+ reputable research firm estimates (2024-2035), break down by technical/channel segments, validate with bottom-up demand proxies, and verify exact HS codes, MFN rates, Section 301 or retaliatory duty stacks, FTA preferential rates (ChAFTA, ACFTA Form E, RCEP), and import VAT/GST across 7-10 candidate countries.
2. **Three-Tier Competitor Landscape**: Verify 15-19 real companies across Tier 1 (global/Western/Japanese leaders), Tier 2 (Chinese export manufacturers), and Tier 3 (overseas private-label/regional resellers) with active URLs, flagship models, specs, pricing benchmarks, MOQs, and positioning gaps.
3. **Compliance, Standards, Export Controls, and Logistics**: Map technical standards driving buyer demand (clearly separating what the product can vs. cannot pass), target-market safety/EMC/environmental rules (CE/Machinery/LVD/EMC/RoHS/EPR, UL/NRTL/FCC, UKCA, RCM, PSE), grid voltage/frequency adaptation, dangerous goods or dual-use export control thresholds (such as UN 3480/38.3 or ECCN 2B116), ocean/air shipping rules (ISPM 15 plywood exemption, Incoterms), and B2B cold-email privacy laws (CAN-SPAM, GDPR/ePrivacy, UK PECR, Australia Spam Act).
4. **Verified Overseas B2B Buyer Directory**: Identify 20-27 real overseas importers, master distributors, private-label brands, or corporate gifting/lab resellers with live URLs, proof they currently sell the category or adjacent lines, pitch angles, and public procurement/contact entry URLs. Never invent contact names, emails, or order volumes.

### 3. Build Auditable Models Without Fabricated Precision

Construct transparent, reproducible calculations before writing the report:

- **Low / Base / High Market Scenarios**: State exact formulas, inputs, and which parameters are assumptions. Use the Base (midpoint) scenario in the main narrative and place Low/High ranges in footnotes or appendices.
- **Weighted Market Selection Matrix**: Score "Country x Buyer Type x Hero SKU" combinations across 6 explicit weighted dimensions (demand, tariffs, compliance/grid fit, channel accessibility, logistics, payment safety) and run sensitivity checks for tariff or certification changes.
- **Unit Economics Model**: Itemize ex-factory cost, export packaging/modification, domestic port charges, international freight/insurance, import duties, after-sales reserve, and customer acquisition cost across FOB vs. DDP scenarios to expose high-tariff traps.

### 4. Structure and Deliver the Report

Write the complete report as a standalone Markdown file (delivered via Canvas) following this executive structure:

1. **One-Page Executive Decision Brief**: Answer only five questions in a compact table: market size (TAM/SAM/SOM), where to enter first, 90-day budget, top risks, and three decisions requiring leadership approval.
2. **Risk Traffic-Light Matrix and Three Approval Items**: Classify key risks as Red/Yellow/Green with owners and mitigations; list three concrete leadership decisions with deadlines and consequences of delay.
3. **Core Chapters (1 to 8)**:
   - Chapter 1: Market Sizing, Scenarios, and Bottom-Up Validation.
   - Chapter 2: Target Country Screening, Top 3 Combinations, Tariff Audit, and Sensitivity.
   - Chapter 3: Competitor Landscape and Top 3 Benchmark Deep Dives.
   - Chapter 4: B2B Buyer Personas and Outreach Priority.
   - Chapter 5: Export Hero SKU Matrix and Fillable Factory Verification Table.
   - Chapter 6: Testing Standards Boundaries, Compliance, Export Controls, Shipping, and Privacy.
   - Chapter 7: B2B Independent Website Architecture, Channel Mix, and High-Intent English Keywords.
   - Chapter 8: Unit Economics (FOB vs. DDP) and 30/60/90-Day Launch Roadmap.
4. **Appendices A to E**:
   - Appendix A: Master Source and Fact-Audit Index (raw figures, years, URLs, limitations).
   - Appendix B: Full Competitor Matrix (15-19 companies).
   - Appendix C: Full Verified Overseas B2B Buyer Directory (20-27 companies).
   - Appendix D: Full Multi-Country Weighted Scoring Table.
   - Appendix E: Rejected Routes and Edge-Case Risk Models.

End every core chapter with a single bold takeaway line starting with `> **本章决策含义：**` (or `> **Chapter Decision Takeaway:**` in English).

## Gotchas

- Never inflate market size by equating the entire upstream industry or third-party testing services market with the factory's addressable hardware market.
- Never claim FOB eliminates destination import tariffs; FOB only shifts customs and duty payment responsibility to the buyer, who still prices landed duty into supplier comparisons.
- Never omit exact raw numbers, currencies, or clickable source URLs, and never invent search volumes, contact persons, or unverified certifications.
- Keep the main report concise by moving full competitor lists, full buyer directories, full scoring tables, and rejected models into Appendices A-E, retaining only Top 3 conclusions in the body.
- Use standard Markdown pipe tables rather than ASCII box-drawing tables so reports render cleanly in Canvas and GitHub.
